How the forecasts changed

The City’s quarterly reports anticipated bringing the project back to the Public Safety Committee in October – December 2025, then January – March 2026, and then April – June 2026. The report covering the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026, gave no committee return date.26

Construction, design approval, and a committee return are distinct milestones. Later forecasts are not completed events.
