Jul 2017

Emergency alcove fencing

Pasadena fenced the restored seating alcoves. A multidisciplinary task force subsequently studied longer-term measures.

Record note An emergency response began while a permanent approach was being developed.

Apr 2018

The Council agreed on a physical barrier

On April 23, the Council unanimously adopted the task force’s direction toward a permanent physical deterrent. Steve Madison moved approval, and Tyron Hampton seconded it.

Record note Policy direction was approved. A finished permanent design was not.

Sep 2018

The emergency fence extended along the bridge

After a prolonged intervention over Labor Day weekend, the City Manager issued an emergency purchase order for full-length temporary fencing.

Record note The emergency measure remained while the design process continued.

May 2019

The first design contract was approved

The Council approved the Donald MacDonald Architects contract after a revised proposal shortened the design schedule by ten months and reduced the proposed contract from $700,000 to $500,000.

Record note The anticipated schedule put Council concept approval in December 2019 and construction in August 2020, pending budget appropriation. These were forecasts, not completed milestones or funded construction commitments.

Feb 2020

Design alternatives and a March–May forecast

The February 2020 presentation compared straight mesh, pickets, curved mesh, and a hybrid. It projected mockup installation in March, commission reviews in April, and a committee recommendation and Council approval in May. At the meeting, Kennedy requested a cost estimate for enclosing the bridge with a roof before Council consideration.

Record note The minutes record that the committee received and filed the information. The March–May dates were forecasts, not completed approvals.

Read the supporting record

The February 3, 2020 presentation separates the task force’s recommended minimum height from the consultant’s broader design criteria. Those criteria included height, resistance to climbing, historic preservation, appearance, and emergency access. Slides 10–27 show the design options and their different dimensions.

Slide 30 ranks curved mesh highest among the options shown, but it does not identify the final mesh type.

Aug 2021

Mockups reached the committee, but no design was selected

After full-scale mockups and advisory reviews, the Public Safety Committee requested further design work, community input, and analysis.

Record note The committee did not recommend a permanent design.

2022–2023

The design process restarted with another consultant

The September 2022 report scheduled a Public Safety Committee return for September 2023. The first request for proposals drew none. A February 2023 newsletter forecast a return by year-end. The City reissued the solicitation and awarded the Apexx Architecture contract on April 14, 2023.

Record note The committee-return forecast had moved from September to the end of 2023 before the new concepts were presented.

Nov 2023

Three new concepts, more questions, and further review

Canted webmesh, vertical webmesh, and metal pickets were presented on November 15. Netting, landscaping, staffing, and substitution questions returned.

Record note The return came after the original September 2023 target but within the later year-end forecast. Commission reviews and a survey continued. No permanent design was selected.

Jan–Jul 2024

Review narrowed the options without a final selection

The Design Commission reached no consensus in January. By July, staff’s options had narrowed to vertical webmesh and metal pickets, and staff returned with patrol costs, netting analysis, rescue concerns, and survey results.

Record note No permanent design was sent to the full Council. Further design work, feedback, and reviews were scheduled.

2025–2026

The design schedule moved again

Capital-project reports moved the design-phase target to June 30, 2028. The report covering work through June 30, 2026 listed continued concept evaluation and an ongoing design phase.

Record note June 2028 was a design-phase target. It was not a funded construction-completion commitment.
