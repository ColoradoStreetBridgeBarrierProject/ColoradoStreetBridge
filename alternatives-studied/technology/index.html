Technology was part of the review from the beginning

Cameras, sensors, phones, and later remote intervention were considered as ways to detect a crisis or help someone respond. The City’s reviewed approach continued to treat these tools as additions to physical protection.

The task force reviewed detection and communication

The reviewed options included cameras, motion sensors, crisis phones, signs, and lighting, alongside physical barriers and patrols.
