Considered as an addition to a barrier

The task force examined planting below the bridge

The 2018 report found no verifying scientific data for using trees below the bridge to cushion a landing or discourage an attempt. It treated trees as a possible addition to a vertical barrier, not a replacement.

2017–2018

Trees were proposed and assessed

Public speakers and Councilmember Tyron Hampton supported planting below the bridge. The task force reviewed the idea alongside fencing, netting, patrols, and technology.

What this does and does not tell us

The report did not find scientific evidence supporting the proposed use of trees instead of a barrier. That does not mean every possible use of landscaping had been tested.
