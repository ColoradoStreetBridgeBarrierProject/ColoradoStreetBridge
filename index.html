Enlarge photographImage source

Why is the fence still there?

In April 2018, the City Council chose to pursue a permanent barrier. The temporary fence installed in September 2018 remains on the bridge.

THE SHORT VERSION

As of September 2026, the records reviewed for this guide do not show an approved permanent design.

The temporary fence stayed while designs went through mockups, commission reviews, public input, a change of consultant, and further study. Construction also required funding separate from the design budget.
