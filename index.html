Colorado Street Bridge
Barrier Enhancements Project

Pasadena’s work since 2017 on a permanent suicide prevention barrier, documented through City reports, meetings, and recordings.

Why is the fence still there?

In April 2018, the City Council chose to pursue a permanent barrier. The temporary fence installed that September still stands in the record reviewed here.

THE SHORT VERSION

Eight years after the Council’s decision, the records reviewed for this guide do not show an approved permanent design. The account has a September 1, 2026 baseline, with later checks identified beside the sources they support.

The temporary fence stayed while designs went through mockups, commission reviews, public input, a change of consultant, and further study. Construction also required funding separate from the design budget.

The record contains both practical delays and repeated reconsideration of alternatives. It does not support treating every delay as the same, or assigning one motive to everyone involved.
